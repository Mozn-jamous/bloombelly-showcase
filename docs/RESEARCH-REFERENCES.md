# Research Foundation & References

BloomBelly is anchored in peer-reviewed clinical and digital-health research. Every feature ties back to documented evidence. This bibliography mirrors the references in the project thesis submitted to Al-Sham Private University.

---

## 1. Fetal Movement Counting

**Saastad, E., Winje, B. A., Stray-Pedersen, B., & Frøen, J. F. (2011).**
*Fetal Movement Counting Improved Identification of Fetal Growth Restriction and Perinatal Outcomes — a Multi-Centre, Randomized, Controlled Trial.*
PLOS ONE, 6(12), e28482.
[DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0028482](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0028482)

> Foundational RCT establishing that systematic kick-counting improves identification of fetal growth restriction. Informs our **kick counter feature** and the ACOG-aligned threshold (10 movements in 2 hours).

---

## 2. Maternal Perception of Fetal Movements

**Weller, K., Housseine, N., Khamis, R. S., Meguid, T., Hofmeyr, G. J., Browne, J. L., & Rijken, M. J. (2023).**
*Maternal perception of fetal movements: Views, knowledge and practices of women and health providers in a low-resource setting.*
PLOS Global Public Health.

> Documents the gap between perceived and clinically significant fetal movement changes in low-resource MENA-adjacent settings. Validates our localized, evidence-based guidance approach.

---

## 3. Reduced Movement Awareness Intervention

**Hayes, D. J. L. (2023).**
*Effect of encouraging awareness of reduced fetal movements.*
American Journal of Obstetrics & Gynecology MFM.

> Reviews interventions that promote maternal awareness of reduced movements. Informs our triage logic for movement-related red flags.

---

## 4. Maternal Perception & Perinatal Outcomes

**Monari, F. (2023).**
*Women's perception of fetal movements and perinatal outcomes.*
Taylor & Francis.

> Establishes the causal chain between perception, action, and outcomes. Supports our emphasis on usable, timely feedback.

---

## 5. Partner Support and Pregnancy Lifestyle

**Versele, V., Bogaerts, A., Devlieger, R., Matthys, C., Gucciardo, L., Deliens, T., et al. (2022).**
*Association between perceived partner support and lifestyle in mother–father dyads expecting a first child.*
Frontiers in Public Health.

> Documents how partner involvement improves maternal lifestyle adherence. Directly informs our **dedicated father section** and the design of partner-engagement content.

---

## 6. Partner Role in Healthy Diet Support

**van Lonkhuijzen, R. M., Rustenhoven, H., de Vries, J. H. M., & Wagemakers, A. (2023).**
*The role of the partner in the support of a pregnant woman's healthy diet: an explorative qualitative study.*
BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth.

> Qualitative study showing that informed partners materially influence dietary choices during pregnancy. Foundation for our nutrition guidance for fathers.

---

## 7. Maternal Nutrition (WHO)
