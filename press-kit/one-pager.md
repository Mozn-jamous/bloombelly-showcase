# BloomBelly — One-Pager

**AI-Assisted, Arabic-First Maternal & Child Health Platform**

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Maternal and child mortality in Arabic-speaking countries remains among the highest in the world. Mothers face a fragmented digital experience: most apps are English-first, translations are mechanical, fathers have no clear role, and information is contradictory across forums and search engines. The result is documented anxiety, missed warning signs, and lost opportunities for intervention during the "first 1,000 days" of life — the highest-leverage health window known.

## ✨ The Solution

**BloomBelly** is a Flutter mobile platform that unifies pregnancy tracking, AI medical image analysis, evidence-based fetal movement monitoring, nutrition evaluation, and a dedicated father section under one Arabic-first interface. Three AI components — Google Gemini for medical images, a LoRA-fine-tuned transformer for the chatbot, and a Random Forest classifier for nutrition — operate behind a Python Flask backend with Supabase persistence.

Distribution is via clinics under a doctor-administered account model with an integrated wallet for premium features.

## 🧬 Evidence-Based

Every feature ties back to peer-reviewed research:

- **Kick counter:** ACOG "Count the Kicks" (Saastad et al., PLOS ONE 2011)
- **Reduced movement awareness:** PLOS Global Public Health, BJOG (2023)
- **Father engagement:** Frontiers in Public Health (2022)
- **Maternal nutrition:** WHO guidelines + King (2016)
- **Pediatric sleep:** American Academy of Sleep Medicine
- **Vaccination:** Syrian Ministry of Health schedules

Full bibliography: 17+ peer-reviewed sources.

- **TAM:** ~5.6M annual pregnant users in the Arab League (smartphone-equipped)
- **SAM (Levant + Gulf + Egypt):** $8M–$15M annual
- **SOM (3-year):** ~$1.6M–$4.5M

## 💰 Business Model

- **B2B2C clinic subscriptions** ($99–$1,499/mo per clinic)
- **Patient wallet** with tiered freemium (core free, AI features paid)
- **Research partnerships** (anonymized opt-in data)
- **Premium content** (postpartum, lactation programs — future)
