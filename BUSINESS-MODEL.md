# Business Model

This document outlines the commercial and sustainability dimensions of BloomBelly — for review by accelerators, investors, and partnership programs evaluating the project's path to market.

---

## Executive Summary

**BloomBelly** is an Arabic-first, AI-assisted maternal and child health platform distributed through clinics on a credit-and-wallet model. We monetize through clinic subscriptions (B2B2C), tiered patient features (B2C freemium), and research partnerships.

The model is designed to be cash-flow positive at modest clinic-adoption scale, with growth capital used to fund product depth and geographic expansion rather than user acquisition.

---

## Market Opportunity

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)

- **Annual pregnancies in the 22-country Arab League:** ~7M
- **Pregnant women in MENA with smartphone access:** estimated 80%+
- **TAM (annual pregnancies × smartphone penetration):** ~5.6M users annually
- **Adjacent: mothers with children under 5:** ~30M

If 10% of these reach an active engagement tier at ARPU $20/year, TAM is **$11M–$60M annually** within Arabic-speaking markets.

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)

Initial focus on:
- **Levant** (Syria, Jordan, Lebanon, Palestine): ~800K annual pregnancies
- **Gulf clinics** (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait): premium tier
- **Egypt:** large population, price-sensitive

Combined SAM: ~$8M–$15M annually at modest adoption.

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM, 3-year view)

- Year 1: 50 clinics, ~2,500 active users, ~$50K–$150K revenue (pilot)
- Year 2: 200 clinics, ~15,000 users, ~$300K–$900K
- Year 3: 800 clinics, ~80,000 users, ~$1.6M–$4.5M

## Value Propositions

### For Pregnant Mothers
- **One source of truth** in Arabic, replacing 5–10 fragmented sources
- **AI-powered convenience** (medical test analysis, instant chatbot answers)
- **Lower anxiety** through evidence-based guidance
- **Continuity from pregnancy to childcare** in one app

### For Clinics
- **Reduced load** from low-acuity information-seeking visits
- **Improved patient adherence** to recommendations
- **Brand differentiation** vs. clinics without digital companion
- **Revenue share** from wallet model
- **Patient data continuity** between visits (with consent)

### For Fathers
- **A structured role** during pregnancy
- **Reduced anxiety** through guided participation
- **A neutral source** for questions they feel awkward asking in person

### For Healthcare Systems
- **Earlier detection** of high-risk pregnancies
- **Reduced cost** of repeated consultations for low-acuity questions
- **Data** (anonymized, opt-in) for population health planning

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Clinic Subscriptions (B2B2C) — Primary

Clinics subscribe at a tiered rate to provide BloomBelly access to their patient panels.

| Tier | Patient Capacity | Monthly Price | Annual Discount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo Practice | 100 patients | $99/mo | $999/yr |
| Group Practice | 500 patients | $399/mo | $3,990/yr |
| Hospital | Unlimited | $1,499/mo | Custom |

Patients of subscribing clinics receive free baseline features and discounted premium features.

### 2. Patient Wallet (B2C Freemium) — Secondary

| Feature | Free? |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy tracking | ✅ Free |
| Kick counter | ✅ Free |
| First-aid guide | ✅ Free |
| Father section | ✅ Free |
| AI chatbot | 💰 $5/month equivalent (wallet) |
| Medical image analysis | 💰 Per-use (wallet) |
| Advanced child tracking | 💰 $3/month equivalent |
| WHO growth charts | 💰 Per-feature |

Patients top up their wallet through their clinic. No in-app payment gateway in V1 (regional regulatory advantage).

### 3. Research Partnerships (Future)

- **Anonymized data export** (opt-in) licensed to research institutions
- **Targeted recruitment** for clinical studies through opt-in user pool
- **Publication-grade insights** sold to public health bodies

Estimated contribution at scale: 10–15% of revenue.

### 4. Premium Content & Courses (Future)

- Childbirth preparation classes
- Lactation consultation packages
- Postpartum mental health programs

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — Pilot (Months 1–6)
- Recruit 5 partner clinics in Damascus
- Onboard 250+ patients
- Document outcomes and testimonials
- Refine the wallet pricing

### Phase 2 — Regional Expansion (Months 6–18)
- Expand to 50 clinics across Syria, Jordan, Lebanon
- Begin Gulf clinic conversations
- Establish first research partnership

### Phase 3 — MENA Scale (Months 18–36)
- 200+ clinics across the Arab League
- App Store and Play Store launch
- Multi-country localization (dialect variants)
- Recurring partnerships with ministries of health

### Phase 4 — Adjacent Markets (Year 3+)
- Expand into pediatric chronic conditions
- B2B partnerships with insurers
- White-label offering for hospital chains

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Slow clinic adoption | Start with founder-network clinics; iterate on pricing |
| Regulatory uncertainty (medical claims) | Doctor-administered model; clear disclaimers; legal review |
| Dependence on Gemini API costs | Multi-vendor AI strategy; LoRA route as cost cap |
| Founder bandwidth | Founders + supervisors are committed full-time post-graduation |
| Geopolitical risks in Syria | Multi-country incorporation; HQ flexibility |

## Why Now

Three converging trends make this the right moment:

1. **AI for healthcare has matured** — multimodal models like Gemini now offer clinical-grade image analysis at accessible price points.
2. **Smartphone penetration in MENA exceeds 80%** — digital distribution finally reaches the patient base.
3. **Post-pandemic demand for digital health is permanent** — clinics that resisted telemedicine in 2019 are early adopters in 2026.

We are positioned to capture this convergence with a product designed from the ground up for our market.
