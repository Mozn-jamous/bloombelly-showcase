## 🤝 Stakeholders & Domain Experts

The project benefited significantly from interviews and consultations with:

- **Obstetricians & Gynecologists** — clinical accuracy review of pregnancy-tracking features
- **Pediatricians** — child growth and vaccination schedule validation
- **Nutritionists** — pregnancy-stage dietary recommendations
- **Mothers** (first-time, second-time, and postpartum) — usability feedback and feature prioritization
- **Fathers** — design input for the partner-support module

Their willingness to share time, expertise, and lived experiences shaped the platform in fundamental ways.
